Practical Ideas to Start With

Each card uses Bootstrap columns to stay clean on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Choose Reusables

Carry a bottle, cloth bag, and lunch box to reduce single-use plastic in everyday routines.

Save Energy

Switch off unused devices, use efficient bulbs, and make the most of natural daylight.

Grow Something

Start with herbs or balcony plants to bring more green space into your home.

Buy Local Food

Choose seasonal produce from nearby markets to support farmers and reduce transport impact.

Sort Your Waste

Separate paper, metal, glass, and food scraps so more materials can be reused or composted.

Travel Smarter

Walk, cycle, carpool, or use public transport whenever possible to lower daily emissions.

Take Action

Pick one habit and begin this week.
